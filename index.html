Why this theme

AI that starts with African context and ends with real community value.

The 2026 theme focuses the event on ownership: African data, African intelligence, and African futures shaped by people building from lived reality rather than importing solutions unchanged.

1

Our Data

Meaningful AI needs data that reflects local languages, environments, institutions, and everyday experience.

2

Our Intelligence

Solutions should be designed, tested, and improved by African researchers, developers, innovators, and communities.

3

Our Future

The goal is measurable impact in healthcare, agriculture, education, finance, public service, and beyond.

Programme

Three days of learning, building, and showcasing Botswana AI.

The programme is being shaped around keynote sessions, practical learning, open speaker submissions, a two-phase hackathon, and a celebratory AI Fest.

Day 1

Community welcome and technical programme

Opening sessions, invited speakers, panels, and practical tracks for participants across academic, industry, startup, and public-sector backgrounds.

Day 2

Workshops, hackathon, and applied sessions

Hands-on learning, hackathon work, mentorship, dataset exploration, and sessions designed to help participants leave with practical skills.

Day 3

AI Fest, posters, demos, and closing

A showcase of student work, research posters, startups, companies, creative AI projects, and community achievements from across Botswana.

Tracks

Built for people who want to learn, contribute, and ship useful work.

Whether you are new to machine learning or already building products, the event creates entry points for learning, collaboration, and public recognition.

Talks

Keynotes, lectures, and tutorials

Sessions across computer vision, data science, multimodal AI, predictive modelling, AI safety, reinforcement learning, NLP, and more.

Hackathon

Data challenges with mentors

A practical challenge pathway with online preparation, in-person building, tutors, judging, and prizes.

Posters

Research and project showcase

Participants are encouraged to bring posters for new ideas, finished projects, early research, community work, and industry applications.

AI Fest

An evening for Botswana's AI builders, artists, startups, and research teams.

AI Fest is designed as a public-facing celebration of local progress, with posters, demos, startup pitches, creative work, and space for companies and research groups to meet talent.

Who should showcase

Students, startups, labs, companies, and independent builders.

Research groups, student teams, AI artists, technology companies, and out-of-school innovators are welcome to share what they are building.

What to expect

Posters, tables, demos, judging, music, and community energy.

The organising team is planning poster boards, presentation tables, startup engagement, judging, and a festive environment for discovery.

Applications

Applications, speaker submissions, posters, and hackathon calls are part of the 2026 flow.

The organising team is coordinating attendee selection, open speaker submissions, hackathon applications, poster submissions, and post-event feedback.
